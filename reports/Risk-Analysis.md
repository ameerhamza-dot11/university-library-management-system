# Risk Analysis

## Project Name
University Library Management System

## Introduction
Risk analysis is the process of identifying possible problems that may affect a project and planning ways to reduce their impact.

## Risk Assessment Table

| No. | Identified Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delay in project completion | High | High | Prepare a proper schedule and complete tasks on time. |
| 2 | Loss of project files | Medium | High | Keep regular backups on GitHub. |
| 3 | Unclear system requirements | Medium | High | Discuss requirements and review the SRS carefully. |
| 4 | Errors in UML diagrams | Medium | Medium | Check diagrams and maintain consistency with requirements. |
| 5 | Limited time for Figma design | High | Medium | Prioritize the five required screens. |
| 6 | Technical problems with tools | Medium | Medium | Learn the tools and keep alternative options available. |
| 7 | Incomplete documentation | Medium | High | Use a checklist to review all required documents. |
| 8 | Negative usability feedback | Low | Medium | Test the prototype and make improvements. |

## Risk Management Strategy

1. Identify possible project risks.
2. Evaluate their probability and impact.
3. Plan actions to reduce each risk.
4. Monitor risks throughout the project.
5. Update the risk assessment when necessary.

## Conclusion
Risk analysis helps identify possible problems before they affect the project. By planning suitable mitigation strategies, the University Library Management System project can be managed more effectively.
